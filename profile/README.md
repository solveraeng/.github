# Solvera

**MEP Engineering. Unleashed.**

At Solvera we develop AI systems to help you design better buildings, faster. Our experienced building engineers plug directly into your projects, and together we define a new standard for MEP engineering in the age of AI.

## What we are building

We are building an engineering firm with AI as its foundation. HVAC, plumbing and electrical engineers decide how a building breathes, where power and water run, and how it is heated and cooled. That work has barely changed in 20 years, while the world needs homes and infrastructure for more than a billion more people.

SolveraOS is the AI system behind every Solvera deliverable:

- **Agents** take on the repetitive engineering work, from sizing airflows to routing ducts and updating circuits, and redo it when the project changes.
- **Analysis and optimization** simulate energy, airflow and loads for each design option, so we deliver the best-performing design and not the first one that works.
- **Engineers in the loop** stay in control, and every deliverable is owned by a licensed engineer.

Read why and how in [our manifesto](https://www.solvera.ai/manifest?lang=en).
